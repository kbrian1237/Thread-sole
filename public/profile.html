Everyday fashion for modern living

Founded in 2023, Thread & Sole was born from a simple idea: that high-quality, sustainable fashion should be accessible for everyone. Our designs are inspired by the urban landscape and the diverse people who inhabit it.

We prioritize organic materials, fair labor practices, and timeless aesthetics. Our pieces are meant to be worn, loved, and passed on. We believe that fashion is not just about what you wear, but how you live.
